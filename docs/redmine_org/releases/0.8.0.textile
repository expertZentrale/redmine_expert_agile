h3. redmine_expert_agile 0.8.0 — 2026-10-13

Epics, as in Jira, on top of Redmine's own subtask tree.

h4. Added

* *Epics.* Choose the trackers whose issues are epics under *Administration → expert Agile → Epics*. An issue belongs to the nearest epic above it in the subtask tree, so a story is a subtask of its epic and the story's subtasks belong to it through the story. No new table and no custom field: Redmine's parent filter, the REST API, the Gantt chart and the story point roll-up see the same data.
* *Epic card field.* With the *Epic* card field switched on, every card on the board and in the backlog that belongs to an epic carries it as a coloured badge, one colour per epic; a story's subtask also names its story, and the tooltip shows the whole path. Only epics and issues the viewer may see are shown.
* *Epics stay off the board* by default, as in Jira. Switching off the board option *Hide epics on the board* brings them back; column counts and totals follow what the board shows.
* *Swimlanes by epic.* One lane per epic plus a lane without one. Dropping a story into another epic's lane makes it that epic's subtask, under the issue form's rules (the *Manage subtasks* permission, the issue's own validation) and into the issue history. A story's subtask and an epic itself are refused by name.
* *Planning into epics.* The backlog planner offers *Epics* next to *Sprints* and *Versions*.
* *Epic progress.* Epic lanes on the board and in the planner, and the epic's issue page, show a bar with the share done — by story points where the work is estimated, by issue count otherwise. A move refreshes every epic it touched.
* *Subtasks card field.* A story's card shows "Subtasks done: 2/5" with a thin bar and folds out into the list of subtasks with their status. Moving a subtask redraws its story's card.
* *Epics tab.* Every open epic of the project as a folding block with its progress, its stories and their subtasks indented beneath; closed epics on request.

h4. Fixed

* *The backlog planner refreshes the lane a card leaves.* Moving a card between two sprints, versions or epics left the source lane's count and story points stale until a reload.

h4. Upgrade notes

No migrations and no new gems. One new setting, *Epic trackers*, defaults to none — epics stay switched off until trackers are chosen. Moving stories between epics needs Redmine's *Manage subtasks* permission. Replace the plugin directory and restart.
